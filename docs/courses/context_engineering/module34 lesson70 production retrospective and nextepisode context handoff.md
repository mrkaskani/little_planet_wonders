# Module 34

## Lesson 70 of 72 — Production Retrospective and Next-Episode Context Handoff

**Estimated study time:** 15 minutes  
**Estimated practice time:** 15 minutes

---

# 1. Learning objective

- Review what succeeded and failed across planning, generation, editing, sound, safety, and education.
- Convert lessons learned into specific project improvements rather than vague opinions.
- Update reusable prompts, Bibles, references, and review rules.
- Prepare a clean handoff for the next episode.
- Create an Episode Retrospective and Next-Episode Handoff.

---

# 2. Why it matters for children under five

A recurring series improves when each completed episode produces better context for the next one. Without a retrospective, the team may repeat the same identity drift, overloaded shots, weak dialogue, missing Foley, unsafe behavior, or review delay.

The retrospective should not be a general statement such as “generation was difficult.” It should identify the failed unit, cause, repair, time cost, and rule that should change. It should also preserve successful patterns.

The next-episode handoff summarizes current canon, approved assets, unresolved issues, reusable references, and known risks so the next production starts from reliable context rather than memory.

# 3. Core concepts

## 1. Retrospective purpose

The goal is to improve the production system, not assign blame.

## 2. What worked

Record successful prompts, references, shot strategies, performance direction, audio methods, and review practices.

## 3. What failed

Record exact failures, their source, and their effect on schedule, continuity, safety, or quality.

## 4. Root cause

Separate symptoms from cause. A distorted hand may come from an overloaded action, weak last frame, or poor contact direction.

## 5. Repair effectiveness

Record which correction solved the problem and whether it introduced new issues.

## 6. Reusable improvement

Turn a lesson into a change to a template, Bible, checklist, reference, naming rule, or workflow.

## 7. Asset promotion

Mark successful images, prompts, voice references, motifs, and shot structures for future reuse.

## 8. Asset retirement

Mark misleading, outdated, drifting, or unsafe references as superseded.

## 9. Unresolved issues

Carry only real unresolved risks or creative questions into the next episode.

## 10. Canon handoff

Summarize approved character knowledge, relationships, props, locations, and world changes.

## 11. Next-episode starting package

Provide current Bibles, approved references, continuity state, available assets, review requirements, and planned learning direction.

## 12. Course progression

The student should now be able to plan and produce a complete episode systematically. The final lessons can use this context in a capstone and series-continuity exercise.

# 4. Practical example from the ongoing series

## Retrospective finding

### Failure

The first pickup prompt combined walking, bending, pickup, showing, and smiling.

### Root cause

The shot was not divided into segments.

### Repair

Created B→C approach, C→D pickup, and D→E show segments.

### Reusable improvement

Add an action-inventory check before every motion prompt.

### Successful asset

The approved C and D boundary frames can be reused for later ball-handling episodes.

### Next-episode note

Pip now knows the phrase “Let’s look again,” but still benefits from visible two-choice comparisons.

# 6. Reusable prompt template

Create an Episode Retrospective and Next-Episode Context Handoff.

Use:
- Failure and Regeneration Logs;
- Shot Review Sheets;
- Picture and audio review reports;
- child-safety review;
- educational review;
- continuity and canon report;
- delivery archive.

Record:
- what worked;
- what failed;
- impact;
- root cause;
- repair;
- reusable improvement;
- assets to promote, revise, or retire;
- remaining risks;
- current canon;
- approved reusable assets;
- unresolved state;
- mandatory future reviews;
- authoritative starting package.

**Important rule**
Convert every major lesson into a specific reusable change. Avoid vague statements such as “be more careful.”

**Output requirements**
Produce a complete retrospective and a clean handoff for the next episode.

---

# 7. Weak prompt

> The episode was good overall. Next time, make the generation more consistent.

---

# 8. Diagnosis of the weak prompt

- “Good overall” gives no evidence.
- Consistency problems are not identified.
- No root cause or repair is recorded.
- Successful assets are not promoted.
- Failed references are not retired.
- Canon and unresolved state are missing.
- The next team receives no authoritative starting package.

---

# 9. Improved prompt

Record specific successes and failures by production area.

For each major failure, identify the exact unit, root cause, correction, and reusable rule. Update the relevant Bible, prompt template, reference, or review checklist.

Promote approved reusable assets and mark outdated or drifting assets as superseded. Summarize current canon, unresolved issues, known risks, mandatory reviews, and authoritative files for the next episode.

---

# 12. Common mistakes

1. Writing vague praise or criticism.
2. Assigning blame.
3. Recording symptoms without causes.
4. Failing to update templates.
5. Keeping outdated references active.
6. Forgetting current canon.
7. Carrying every minor concern forward.
8. Starting the next episode from memory.

---

# 13. Fifteen-minute practical exercise

Create a retrospective for the red-ball episode.

1. Record five things that worked.
2. Record five failures or risks.
3. Identify root causes.
4. Record repairs and results.
5. Update at least three reusable project documents.
6. Promote and retire assets.
7. Summarize canon.
8. Create the next-episode handoff.

# 15. Short quiz and answers

### Question 1

What is the purpose of a retrospective?

### Answer

To improve the production system, not assign blame.
### Question 2

What should follow a root-cause finding?

### Answer

A specific reusable improvement.
### Question 3

What is asset promotion?

### Answer

Marking approved material for future reuse.
### Question 4

What is asset retirement?

### Answer

Marking outdated, drifting, unsafe, or misleading material as superseded.
### Question 5

What should the next episode start from?

### Answer

The authoritative handoff package, not memory.
