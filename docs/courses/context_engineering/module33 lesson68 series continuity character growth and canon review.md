# Module 33

## Lesson 68 of 72 — Series Continuity, Character Growth, and Canon Review

**Estimated study time:** 15 minutes  
**Estimated practice time:** 15 minutes

---

# 1. Learning objective

- Compare the final episode with permanent series, character, world, visual, audio, and relationship context.
- Approve only deliberate changes to character knowledge, emotion, relationships, props, or locations.
- Prevent accidental production differences from entering canon.
- Update the Continuity Ledger and approved project documents responsibly.
- Create a Series Continuity and Canon Report.

---

# 2. Why it matters for children under five

Each completed episode may add new information to the series. Pip may learn to compare colors more carefully. Lumi and Pip may establish a new turn-taking habit. A repaired garden sign may remain repaired.

Other differences should not continue. A slightly different hair shape, changed scarf color, moved doorway, altered voice, or accidental prop design is not character growth or world development.

Canon review decides what becomes true in future episodes. It compares the final media with authoritative project context and records approved updates.


## Recurring example series

All examples continue the same evolving preschool series:

# Lumi and Pip’s Little Garden

- **Lumi:** a curious four-year-old child with warm brown skin, a dark curly bob, coral overalls, a cream shirt, yellow boots, and a leaf-shaped hair clip.
- **Pip:** a small rounded teal-blue bird with a cream belly, orange beak, sky-blue scarf, large expressive eyes, gentle hops, and short calm flights.
- **Auntie Noura:** a patient adult guide with warm medium-brown skin, dark hair in a low bun, a sage-green gardening apron, a plum shirt, and calm reassuring movement.
- **World:** Sunseed Garden, including the Rainbow Garden, Cozy Workshop, and Little Pond Path.
- **Visual identity:** rounded 2D cut-paper design, soft texture, simple facial features, clear silhouettes, stable eye-level camera, warm light, and uncluttered backgrounds.
- **Audio identity:** warm voices, short sentences, restrained music, clear ambience, gentle Foley, and protected quiet space.

# 3. Core concepts

## 1. Canon

Canon is the approved set of facts, identities, relationships, knowledge, events, and world changes that remain true for the series.

## 2. Identity continuity

Character appearance, voice, personality, movement, and relationship identity should match approved references.

## 3. Knowledge growth

A character may gain an approved concept or strategy. Future episodes should respect that knowledge.

## 4. Emotional growth

A character may become more comfortable asking for help or trying again. Growth should remain age-appropriate and gradual.

## 5. Relationship growth

Shared experiences may strengthen trust, turn-taking, or cooperation. The update should be specific.

## 6. Prop and location change

A prop may be repaired, moved permanently, or introduced as recurring. A location may gain an approved stable feature.

## 7. Temporary episode state

Weather, dirt, held props, temporary clothing, and one-scene emotion normally do not become permanent.

## 8. Accidental drift

Unapproved visual, audio, or behavioral variation must be rejected or ignored as canon.

## 9. Contradiction review

Check whether the episode conflicts with earlier knowledge, world rules, relationships, or location geography.

## 10. Write-back process

Only approved changes are written into the Character Bible, Relationship Bible, Prop Bible, Location Bible, World Bible, or series timeline.

## 11. Future-episode note

Record what later writers should remember and what should not be repeated identically.

## 12. Version authority

Keep dated or versioned records so later teams know which context is current.

> “Let’s look again.”

### Not canon

- A slightly darker blue scarf in one generated shot
- A moved garden bench
- A changed leaf hair clip
- A temporary red ball holder state

# 6. Reusable prompt template

Review the completed episode for series continuity and canon.

Compare final media with:
- Series Bible;
- Character Bibles;
- Relationship Bible;
- World Bible;
- Location Bibles;
- Prop Bible;
- Wardrobe Bible;
- Visual, Color, Lighting, Camera, Voice, Music, and Sound Bibles;
- prior continuity records.

Identify:
- identity matches;
- contradictions;
- approved knowledge growth;
- emotional growth;
- relationship growth;
- approved prop, location, or world changes;
- temporary state that ends;
- unresolved state that continues;
- accidental drift that must be rejected;
- project documents requiring update.

**Output requirements**
Produce a Series Continuity and Canon Report with human approval fields.

---

# 7. Weak prompt

> Use anything that looks good in the final episode as the new official design.

---

# 8. Diagnosis of the weak prompt

- Attractiveness replaces continuity authority.
- Accidental drift may become permanent.
- Character and world identity may change episode by episode.
- Temporary state may be mistaken for canon.
- Growth and redesign are confused.
- Earlier project documents become unreliable.
- No approval or version process exists.

---

# 9. Improved prompt

Compare the final episode with approved project documents.

Write forward only deliberate approved changes such as new character knowledge, emotional coping, relationship development, repaired props, or authorized location changes.

Reject accidental visual, voice, wardrobe, layout, color, or behavior drift. Close temporary episode states. Carry unresolved story state only when intentional.

Update the relevant Bibles and Continuity Ledger only after human continuity and creative approval.

---

# 12. Common mistakes

1. Treating visual variation as redesign.
2. Forgetting knowledge growth.
3. Repeating a resolved conflict unchanged.
4. Leaving temporary weather or clothing active.
5. Updating the Series Bible for minor episode state.
6. Failing to record unresolved details.
7. Using an outdated Bible.
8. Skipping human continuity approval.

---

# 15. Short quiz and answers

### Question 1

What is canon?

### Answer

The approved facts and changes that remain true for the series.
### Question 2

Does attractive drift become canon automatically?

### Answer

No.
### Question 3

What may become approved growth?

### Answer

Knowledge, emotional skill, relationships, props, locations, or world changes.
### Question 4

What happens to temporary state?

### Answer

It is closed unless intentionally carried forward.
### Question 5

When are Bibles updated?

### Answer

After deliberate human approval.
