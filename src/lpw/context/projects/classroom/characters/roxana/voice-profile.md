# Roxana voice profile

Roxana is a warm, gentle, clear young-adult caregiver voice with medium-low
pitch, slow-to-moderate speech, readable articulation, natural pauses, and a calm,
curious, reassuring baseline. Emotional intensity never exceeds moderate. Preserve
vocal age, accent, warmth, pace, and identity across every episode.

Canonical structured settings live in `audios/voices/roxana.yaml`.
