# Voice production rules

Emotional references preserve permanent character identity but are never final
shot audio unless they contain the exact approved words. Every speaking shot uses
new clean dialogue, reviewed for exact wording, pronunciation, identity, comfort,
and emotional safety. Only locked dialogue may drive lip synchronization.

Dialogue files contain no music, ambience, Foley, or sound effects. Material
changes after locking require a new dialogue version and regenerated lip sync.
